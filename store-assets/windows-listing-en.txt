Font V is an offline font explorer for Windows. Browse the fonts installed on your PC, preview your own text, and find the right typeface for your next design.

• Browse font families and styles with search and an alphabetical index.
• Compare 2–4 fonts using the same text and typography settings.
• Adjust size, letter spacing, line height, alignment, and text/background colors.
• Check character coverage, including a filter for modern Hangul syllables.
• Save favorites and organize fonts into named collections.
• Export PNG previews with optional font information and transparency.
• Import TTF/OTF files into app-local storage without installing fonts system-wide.
• Choose English or Korean and one of five color themes.

Your font catalog, imported fonts, text, favorites, and settings stay on your device. Windows has no ads, account requirement, or in-app purchases. Font availability varies with the fonts installed on your PC. Imported font files are not licensed or distributed by Font V; use fonts according to their owners' licenses.

Requires Windows 10 version 1809 or later, x64.
